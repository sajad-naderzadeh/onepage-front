Best Lemon Recipes

By: Author Barry C. Parsons

Posted on Published: March 22, 2019

Categories Archives, Cakes, Pies, and Tarts, Dessert Recipes, Dinner Recipes, Recipes

Best Lemon Recipes. A countdown of the 50 most popular sweet and savoury lemon recipes here on Rock Recipes over the past decade.

It’s late March and we are holding out for Spring soon. I’m not really sure about what the connection is between the lead up to the Spring/Easter season and lemon recipes.  Over the years it seems to get stronger.

Perhaps it’s the bright fresh flavour of lemon that makes folks think of spring. Maybe it’s a natural scurvy prevention reaction. 🙂 I’m not sure.

Lemon.

One thing for certain though, we always see a jump in the popularity of lemon recipes at this time of year. So, with that in mind I thought I’d take a look at the numbers and see which recipes have been most popular on this website over the past 11 years.

There are over 100 lemon recipes on the site in all. So there’s plenty more lemon deliciousness to discover. And plenty more to come in the future.

You’re sure to find several you’ll want to make over and over again! Enjoy lemon lovers!
